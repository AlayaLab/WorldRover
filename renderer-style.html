TL;DR
  • A rendering engine that turns artist-built UE worlds into annotated video.
  • 10M+ frames with 6 pixel-aligned modalities from a single render per frame.
  • Any route re-observed from 3 viewpoints and any appearance — scene & motion held fixed.

Viewpoints

Multimodal · one render per frame

Switch the modality, keep the pixel

Colour, depth and motion are the same rasterization of the same instant. Toggle a channel to see how each stream is presented. Depth / flow panels are illustrative previews.

RGB illustrative

Photorealistic colour under dynamic Lumen global illumination — first/third-person at 1280×720, panoramic at 4096×2048.

Appearance as a variable

Same geometry & motion, any climate

Because lighting is simulated at render time rather than baked, re-rendering an instant under another state changes the image physically. The same first-person frame, five states:

Day
Day
Sunset
Night
Firelit
Fog

White model. The same frame re-rendered with every surface in the engine's default material — geometry and occlusion survive at every scale while texture, colour and emissive light do not. The coarse-to-real supervision a generative renderer trains against.

Three camera viewpoints

One walk, seen three ways

First-person

Egocentric

The camera is the agent, so imagery, pose and the action stream are consistent by definition.

Third-person

Following an actor

An animated character replays the path while a follow camera moves independently — ground truth for both.

360° panoramic

Whole-scene

4096×2048 equirectangular, assembled from six perspective cube faces in linear light.

Panoramic assembly. Six 100° cube faces rendered tone-mapping-off share one radiometric scale, reprojected to equirectangular, feather-blended and re-exposed once — a seamless linear-HDR panorama.

Diversity & scale

Film-grade content, multiplied by combinatorics

Scene library. Fifteen of 30+ Unreal Engine environments — waterfront city, transit interior, cathedral, alpine meadow, cyberpunk street, Paris street, jungle ruin, and more — all under dynamic Lumen GI.

Applications

Built for three problems, on the same pixels

01

Interactive world models

RGB, depth and the recorded camera + action stream — camera conditioning is exact, and third-person separates camera from subject.

02

Dynamic 4D reconstruction

Dense flow and long-range tracks through occlusion over an articulated subject and an independently moving camera.

03

Generative rendering

The white model pairs coarse structure with the finished image frame-for-frame, extended across day, night, fog and snow.
